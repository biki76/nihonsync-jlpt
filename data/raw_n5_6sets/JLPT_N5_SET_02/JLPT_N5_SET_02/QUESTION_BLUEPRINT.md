# QUESTION BLUEPRINT (practice-paper specification)

These counts are PRACTICE BENCHMARKS, not official JLPT counts. They are set from the official
category matrix (OFFICIAL_FORMAT_REFERENCE.md) plus the author's knowledge of the typical
shape of released sample papers; they could not be cross-checked against the official sample
PDFs in this environment. They stay constant across all 20 sets of a level.
